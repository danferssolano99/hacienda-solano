# Arquitecto Composition Archetypes

Pick one archetype before placing objects.

## 1. Social Hearth

Use when the emotional center is conversation, warmth, and lingering.

- Primary focal point: fogatera
- Support focal point: planting frame or view edge
- Seating: inward-facing
- TV: secondary or off-axis
- Best for: intimate patios, evening zones, compact sharing spaces

## 2. Viewing Terrace

Use when the screen or outward view leads the arrangement.

- Primary focal point: TV or long view
- Support focal point: fire or table
- Seating: front-facing with conversation compromise
- Planting: side framing, not front obstruction
- Best for: sports, movie, and lounge viewing

## 3. Dining Nook

Use when eating is the main event and all other elements support it.

- Primary focal point: table
- Support focal point: grill or garden edge
- Seating: table-centered
- TV: optional and peripheral
- Best for: breakfast terraces, compact grills, two-person outdoor dining

## 4. Framed Corner Retreat

Use when the site is small and needs edge control more than central symmetry.

- Primary focal point: one anchored object near a boundary
- Support focal point: opposite corner softening
- Seating: diagonal or L-like reading
- Planting: used to absorb hard corners
- Best for: 4x4 to 5x5 zones, leftover terraces, awkward rectangles
